Organised by the three parts of the talk, from one person to a group to a society. Each entry gives its title, authors, year, venue and a link. Every entry was checked against Crossref, arXiv, OpenReview or the official proceedings page (first on 2026-10-13, again in full on 2026-10-25); where a paper exists both on arXiv (or a similar server) and in a journal or proceedings, the published version is listed. Links only; titles as published. Learner modelling and knowledge tracing are out of scope (a separate review covers them). In the three dataset lists, each entry gives how to obtain it (public, registration, application or restricted) and its licence as stated on the official page (checked 2026-10-13); "licence unclear" means that page names none.

- **GlobalOpinionQA** — Pew Global Attitudes + WVS items with country distributions. *Public (Hugging Face); CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.* [link](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anthropic/llm_global_opinions)
- **World Values Survey (Wave 7)** — Cross-national values survey. *Registration; own conditions of use (non-commercial, no redistribution).* [link](https://www.worldvaluessurvey.org)
- **General Social Survey** — US social attitudes and behaviour since 1972. *Public; own terms of use (no named licence).* [link](https://gss.norc.org)
- **Psych-101** — Trial-level data from many psychology experiments in natural language. *Public (Hugging Face); Apache 2.0.* [link](https://huggingface.co/datasets/marcelbinz/Psych-101)
- **SubPOP** — Subpopulation response distributions from large surveys. *Application (gated, manual approval); data CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, code BSD-3-Clause.* [link](https://github.com/JosephJeesungSuh/subpop)
- **Twin-2K-500** — Individual answers to 500+ questions (personality, cognition, economics, experiments). *Public (Hugging Face); CC BY 4.0.* [link](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LLM-Digital-Twin/Twin-2K-500)
- **Many Labs 2** — Multi-site replication of classic effects. *Public (OSF); CC0 1.0.* [link](https://doi.org/10.1177/2515245918810225)
- **MovieLens** — User movie ratings. *Public; own usage licence (research use, no commercial use without permission).* [link](https://doi.org/10.1145/2827872)
- **PersonaChat (ParlAI)** — Persona-conditioned crowdworker dialogues. *Public; data CC BY 4.0, code MIT.* [link](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ParlAI)
